01

Information We Collect

Wallpypers is designed to work with as little personal information as reasonably necessary.

Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, you may voluntarily provide information such as your preferences, favorite wallpaper categories, or other information you choose to submit within the app.

Information collected automatically

We may collect limited technical information required to operate, maintain, and improve the service, including:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App version and basic technical information
  • Usage and interaction information
  • Crash and diagnostic information

We do not ask for sensitive personal information simply to browse or discover wallpapers.

How We Use Information

Information we collect may be used to:

  • Provide and operate Wallpypers

  • Remember your preferences and favorites
  • Improve app performance and user experience

  • Protect the service from misuse or abuse

We do not use your information for purposes unrelated to the operation and improvement of Wallpypers without an appropriate legal basis or your consent where required.

Wallpypers does not claim ownership of third-party copyrighted material unless explicitly stated. Copyright and other intellectual property rights remain with their respective owners.

Where appropriate, we may provide attribution or a reference to the original source.

If you believe that content available through Wallpypers infringes your copyright or other rights, you can contact us so the matter can be reviewed and appropriate action can be taken.

Storage & Downloads

When you download a wallpaper, the file may be saved to your device according to the permissions and storage settings provided by your operating system.

Wallpypers does not automatically gain access to other files stored on your device simply because you download a wallpaper.
